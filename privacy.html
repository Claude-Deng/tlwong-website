Privacy.

How the firm handles personal information shared through this website — and what this website does not collect.

Last updated · November 2026

This notice explains how TL Wong & Co. (“the firm”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information that is shared with us through this website. It should be read together with the terms of any engagement between the firm and its clients.

What this website collects

This website is a set of static pages. It does not run analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not create a profile of your visit. The enquiry form does not transmit anything to a server: it prepares an email in your own email application, which you choose whether to send.

If you choose to contact us, we will receive the information you include in that email or call — typically your name, company, contact details and a description of the matter.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and to decide whether we can assist.
  • To manage an existing engagement, including correspondence and records.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and professional obligations that apply to the firm.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Sharing

Information is shared only where it is needed to provide the service, where you have asked us to, or where the law or a professional obligation requires it. This may include service providers who support the firm’s email, document storage and IT systems, and the firm’s professional advisers.

Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it was collected, or for the period required by law and professional standards. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, and we agree the appropriate channel before sensitive documents are exchanged. Email is not a secure channel for confidential material; please do not send sensitive documents to a general email address.

Your choices

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask us to stop contacting you. To make a request, email dr.tlwong@outlook.com or call +60 12-335 1466. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Links to other sites

This website links to the firm’s client portal and to mapping services. Those sites are operated by third parties and have their own privacy notices. This notice does not cover them.
