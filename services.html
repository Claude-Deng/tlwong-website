The practice

We not only know the numbers. We understand the business environment and the pace at which it changes — and we bring the technical disciplines together around a single commercial question.

Each engagement starts with the issue as management sees it. From there, we set out the requirements, the options and the consequences in plain terms, then stay involved through the execution.

Planning & restructuring

For founders and management teams shaping what comes next.

Discuss this service

Building a Business Plan

We not only know the numbers; we understand the business environment and its fast-changing operating conditions. Our team can plan for new businesses, restructure existing ones and prepare the business plan that moves the business forward.

A business plan is more than a document for a lender or an investor. It is the discipline of testing whether the model works — the market, the cost base, the capital required and the sequence of decisions that follow. We help management build that case and pressure-test it before it is presented.

  • New business planning
  • Business restructuring
  • Business plan preparation

Tax advisory & investigation

Discuss this service

Taxation Advisory and Investigation

Our team of tax professionals brings the experience not only to ensure full compliance, but to walk with you through the tax planning — and, where necessary, a tax investigation. We hold the process through each stage and work to mitigate the exposure.

Good tax advice sits between the statute and the commercial reality. It requires a clear view of the transactions, the documentation and the timing, and a willingness to explain the position in language management can act on.

  • Tax compliance
  • Tax planning
  • Investigation support

Audit, assurance & forensic

When the numbers have to stand up to scrutiny.

Discuss this service

Auditing and Forensic Investigation

Financial reporting in the corporate world is ever evolving, and the ability to relate it to the business environment raises the bar. Our audit and assurance professionals articulate the financial reporting requirements that match the business — not a generic template.

Our team is also experienced in forensic investigation for company fraud and insurance industry requirements. That work demands a different discipline: evidence, chronology and a report that can withstand challenge.

  • Audit & assurance
  • Financial reporting
  • Forensic investigation

Capital & funding

For companies with a plan that needs capital behind it.

Discuss this service

Corporate Finance Advisory Services

In business, the biggest challenge is often finding the capital to invest, structuring the funding requirements and sourcing the right funding partner. We provide services for funding planning across that gap — from start-ups to international and multinational companies.

We are also experienced in initial public offerings in international markets and in the compliance work that follows. The objective is not simply a transaction; it is a capital structure the business can live with.

  • Funding strategy
  • Capital sourcing
  • IPO readiness & compliance

05

M&A and restructuring

For the moments when the shape of the business has to change.

Discuss this service

Merger & Acquisition / Corporate Restructuring Services

During a downturn or recession, companies that have over-traded can move into serious loss and need to downsize or restructure to stay in the game. That requires difficult, painful actions and execution — and management may find it hard to lead alone. We do the work that has to be done, with a clear plan.

In good times, companies need to grow and capture the market at the fastest speed possible. We provide strategic planning and execution, source potential candidates and negotiate — with experience in cross-border mergers and acquisitions and complex global deals.

  • Strategic planning
  • Target sourcing & negotiation
  • Cross-border M&A
