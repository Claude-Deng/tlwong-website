We are a professional practising firm of Chartered Accountants, established in Malaysia since 1989. We advise on business-related matters including auditing, taxation and forensic investigation, and company secretarial matters — from the basic start-up to the most complex company structure, in a fast-changing international operating environment.

We are a team of highly experienced professionals in our respective fields, able to make significant changes to improve business opportunities and the environment in which they operate.
