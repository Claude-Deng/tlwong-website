Established in Malaysia in 1989, TL Wong & Co. is a professional practising firm of Chartered Accountants. We advise businesses at every stage — from the first plan to the most complex structure.

01 / Our story

We are a professional practising firm of Chartered Accountants, established in Malaysia since 1989. Our work covers auditing, taxation advisory and forensic investigation, company secretarial matters, and business advisory — from the basic start-up to the most complex company structure.

We work in a fast-changing international operating environment, across the globe. That means the technical answer is only part of the job: it has to fit the business, the people running it and the market they operate in.

We are a team of highly experienced professionals in our respective fields, able to make significant changes to improve business opportunities and the environment in which our clients operate.
